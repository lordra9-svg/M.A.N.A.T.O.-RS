# Provenance and licensing notes

M.A.N.A.T.O.-RS is an independent Rust implementation of the M.U.G.E.N runtime.
It is **not** a port of M.U.G.E.N and contains no M.U.G.E.N source code,
binaries, or game assets.

> Not affiliated with Elecbyte. M.U.G.E.N is a trademark of Elecbyte.

## Current source policy

M.U.G.E.N itself is closed source. The current strict-clean-room policy allows:

1. **Ikemen-GO** (https://github.com/ikemen-engine/Ikemen-GO, MIT licensed) as
   the primary engine reference and black-box oracle.
2. **Public format documentation and community knowledge** for file layouts,
   trigger/controller semantics, and the coordinate system.
3. **Stock and user-provided content** as input data. Content is read in place
   from the user's M.U.G.E.N installation and is not copied into the engine.
4. **Derived black-box assertions** recorded from Ikemen-GO behavior. Raw
   oracle traces and screenshots are not committed.

No M.U.G.E.N binaries are used as an oracle. No decompilation or disassembly is
performed.

## Historical note

An early prototype consulted `mugen-reimpl`, a C reference whose README states
that its architecture was derived from REA/Ghidra analysis of M.U.G.E.N 1.1b1,
1.0, and WinMUGEN Plus binaries. That checkout has been deleted. The subsystems
influenced by it are listed in `CLEANROOM_SPEC.md` and are scheduled for
rewrite from the frozen MIT/docs specification. No `mugen-reimpl` code or binary
is used going forward.

## Clean-room status

The project now follows a spec-first process: subsystem behavior is written in
`docs/spec/` from allowed sources, frozen, and then implemented. Because the
same contributors write the specification and the implementation, this is not a
legally separated clean room; that limitation is recorded in
`CLEANROOM_SPEC.md` rather than hidden.

## Provenance of specific decisions

- **SFF v1/v2** was rewritten from `docs/spec/sff.md`, derived from Ikemen-GO
  (MIT), public format documentation, and content behavior. The old
  reference-influenced parser is gone. SFF v1 uses embedded PCX images with
  inline palettes; the PCX RLE stream is decompressed into
  `bytes_per_line * height` and then tightened to `width`.
- **SND** was rewritten from `docs/spec/snd.md`; **AIR** was rewritten from
  `docs/spec/air.md`; **CNS/CMD** parsing and command matching were rewritten
  from `docs/spec/cns-cmd.md`. All three specs are derived from Ikemen-GO,
  public documentation, and observed content.
- **SFF v2** indexed sprites (RLE8, RLE5, LZ5) and PNG sprites begin with a
  four-byte little-endian uncompressed-size field. PNG8 sprites are indexed
  images whose own `PLTE` may be a dummy; the real colors come from the SFF
  palette node, so the decoder keeps raw indices and maps them itself.
- **Statedef defaults** follow the CNS documentation: `type = S`,
  `movetype = I`, `physics = N`; an explicit `U` means "unchanged". Entering
  state 0 therefore clears an attack/hit move type, which the CPU uses to decide
  when it may act.
- **Landing** from any ordinary `physics = A` state transitions to state 52 when
  it exists, matching the observed behavior of stock air attacks that do not
  carry their own `ChangeState` on landing.
- **Animation timing** treats `-1` as "display forever" and reports `AnimTime`
  as ticks remaining, with one tick at `total_time` before wrapping so
  `AnimTime = 0` triggers fire.

## Assets and licensing

- The engine code and tools are MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`).
- The bundled characters in `content/chars/kaen`, `content/chars/nival`, and
  `content/chars/ordan` are original works by the M.A.N.A.T.O.-RS contributors
  and are MIT licensed. Their `common.cns` is an original minimal common-state
  file, not a copy of Elecbyte's `common1.cns`.
- The bundled character sound banks are original synthesized effects generated
  by `tools/gen_sfx.py`; they are MIT licensed with the characters.
- The bundled Training Grid stage, fight effects, and common fight sounds in
  `content/` are original procedural assets generated by
  `tools/gen_assets.py` and are MIT licensed.
- No stock M.U.G.E.N content is distributed with this repository. Stock
  characters, stages, sprites, sounds, and scripts can be loaded from a
  user-supplied root and remain the property of their original authors.
- Third-party Rust dependencies retain their own licenses. Run
  `cargo deny check licenses` (or `cargo license`) before redistributing a
  binary.

## What was deliberately not done

- This project did not decompile or disassemble M.U.G.E.N executables.
- No `mugen-reimpl` C functions were copied into Rust; its checkout is deleted.
- No stock or unlicensed copyrighted sprites, sounds, scripts, or stages were bundled.
- No attempt was made to replicate undocumented engine bugs; compatibility is
  driven by content behavior and public semantics.

## Publishing notes

- Keep any external/stock content directory out of the repository.
- The bundled characters, sound banks, fight effects, common sounds, and
  Training Grid stage are all MIT licensed.
- Do not claim legally separated clean-room status until the subsystems listed in
  `CLEANROOM_SPEC.md` have been rewritten and reviewed. The current status is
  spec-first with a documented historical reference.
